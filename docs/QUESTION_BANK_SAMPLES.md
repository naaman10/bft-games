## Answer Format Guidelines

### Numeric Answers
- Accept exact matches: `"20"`, `"42"`
- Handle decimals: `"3.14"`, `"5.2"`
- Handle fractions: `"1/2"`, `"3/4"`

### Special Cases
- Ratios: `"2:3"`, `"1:4"`
- Percentages: Accept with or without % symbol: `"25"` or `"25%"`
- Multiple valid forms: For fractions, accept equivalent forms (e.g., "1/2" = "2/4")

## Question Difficulty Levels

### Level 1 (Easy)
- Basic operations
- Small numbers
- Simple concepts

### Level 2 (Medium)
- Multi-step problems
- Larger numbers
- Mixed operations

### Level 3 (Hard)
- Complex problems
- Word problems
- Advanced concepts

## Expansion Strategy

### Phase 1 (Initial Launch)
- 50 questions per subject per year group
- Focus on core subjects: Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication, Division

### Phase 2 (Month 2)
- 100 questions per subject per year group
- Add: Fractions, Decimals, Percentages

### Phase 3 (Month 3)
- 200+ questions per subject per year group
- Add: Geometry, Time, Money, Measurement
- Word problems for all subjects

### Long-term
- 500+ questions per subject per year group
- Adaptive difficulty
- Context-aware question selection
- User-generated content (teacher submitted questions)
